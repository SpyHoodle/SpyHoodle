> $\texttt{Passion has little to do with euphoria and everything to do with patience. It is not about feeling good. It is about endurance.}$
> $\texttt{Like patience, passion comes from the same Latin root: pati. It does not mean to flow with exuberance.}$
>
> $\texttt{It means to suffer.}$

― $\text{Zampanò, } \textit{\color{blue}House} \textit{ of Leaves}$.
